# Customer-facing output format

> **This is a starter skill** demonstrating team-scoped sharing. Customer-ops
> owns it — edit it to encode how we actually want customer-facing outputs to
> look, and every teammate on the team picks up the change automatically.

When asked to produce something a customer will see, follow this house style:

## Structure
1. **Headline** — one line stating the outcome or ask, no preamble.
2. **Context** — 2–3 sentences max.
3. **Details** — bullets, not paragraphs. Lead each with the takeaway.
4. **Next step** — exactly one clear action with an owner and a date.

## Voice
- Plain, direct, warm. No jargon, no hedging.
- Numbers get context ("up 12% week-over-week"), never bare.
- Never expose internal tooling names, ticket IDs, or Slack handles.

## Don't
- No walls of text. No "I hope this email finds you well."
- Don't promise dates you can't source.
