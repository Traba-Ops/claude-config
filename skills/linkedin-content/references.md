# linkedin-content — sources & provenance

For maintainers, not loaded at runtime. LinkedIn's ranking is opaque and shifts often, so treat every magnitude below as *directional* and **re-verify these before making substantive edits to `SKILL.md`.**

**Researched:** 2026-07-01.

## Sources

- [LexiConn — "Why Motivational LinkedIn Posts Are Dying in 2026"](https://www.lexiconn.in/knowledge/blogs/motivational-linkedin-posts-dying-2026.html)
  → the evidence-over-emotion shift; ~40–65% reach drop for low-effort motivational content.
- [LinkBoost — "LinkedIn Organic Reach 2026: Beat the New AI Algorithm"](https://www.linkboost.co/blog/linkedin-organic-reach-2026)
  → the "360 Brew" ranking model; the ~90-minute test on a slice of your network, expanded or killed on early quality signals; dwell time + substantive comments/saves over likes.
- [LinkBoost — "LinkedIn Algorithm Changes 2026: Beat the Depth Score"](https://www.linkboost.co/blog/linkedin-algorithm-changes-2026/)
  → the "depth score"; penalty for empty broetry / length-inflating formatting; hook length guidance.
- [Gallium — "Founder-Led LinkedIn Content Strategy in 2026"](https://gallium.ai/blog/founder-led-linkedin-content-strategy-2026)
  → format→goal matching (story = trust, teardown = specificity, framework = saves, opinion = positioning); the four proof types (problem / market / execution / judgment); reply-in-the-first-hour; no links in body; ~2 posts/week cadence.
- [Maria Marchewka — "Contrarian Hook" post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/maria-marchewka_linkedin-growth-isnt-about-the-flashy-tactics-activity-7369366605954260992-Qopb)

- [Peter Bencsik — "LinkedIn 'Broetry'"](https://peterbencsik.com/blog/linkedin-broetry/)
  → why the broetry/algorithm-baiting era is fading.

## Note on the "link in the first comment" rule

Widely-followed creator practice, corroborated across the founder-strategy sources above (external links in the post body suppress reach). The *size* of the penalty is debated and LinkedIn has softened it at times — but the tactic costs nothing, so it stays a default.
